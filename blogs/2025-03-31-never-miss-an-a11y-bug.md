Web accessibility (A11y) is crucial for creating inclusive applications that serve all users, regardless of their abilities. The term “A11y” (pronounced “a-double-one-y”) stands for accessibility. Web accessibility is covered by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and generally should meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) standards.
