When partnering with an outside vendor, especially in financial technology, the stakes are high. Investment advisors need secure, reliable solutions for their IT infrastructure, making the selection process a critical component of maintaining client trust and driving operational efficiency. This is challenging enough, but the rate at which software tools change and cybersecurity risks grow greatly exacerbates the situation.
