When we talk internally about Opto’s approach and edge, we like to say that we are the product of a successful marriage between Silicon Valley software engineering and cutting-edge high finance. This marriage of intellectual heritages is well exemplified by two of Opto’s co-founders; Jacob Miller brings his experience at Bridgewater Associates and Matt Reed his tenure at Addepar. Here we’ll explore some of the major philosophical pillars, along with how they weave together to create the new fabric of Opto.
