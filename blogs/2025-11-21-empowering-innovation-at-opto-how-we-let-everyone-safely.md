At Opto, we believe great ideas can come from anywhere, not just from engineers. Whether you’re in product, design, finance, or marketing, if you’ve got a spark and want to ship something, you should be able to open a pull request (PR) and get it out into the world. But moving fast shouldn’t mean breaking production.
