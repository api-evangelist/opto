Demos are more than just walkthroughs of software. Done correctly, they are powerful narratives, carefully crafted to resonate with your audience, solve their problems, and leave a lasting impression. Whether you’re showcasing your product to a potential client, an investor, or a new hire, every demo should be as unique as the person watching it .
