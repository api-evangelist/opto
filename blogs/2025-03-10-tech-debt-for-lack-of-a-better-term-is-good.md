“We don’t want to create any tech debt .” This was the answer to me asking if we’d shipped a feature yet. What they meant by that statement was that they didn’t want to make a mistake; they didn’t want to build the thing that engineers spend the next five years complaining about. But I would argue a different mistake was made.
